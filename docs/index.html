Privacy Policy

In short: the «Oliy maqsad» app collects, stores and transmits no personal data whatsoever. The app has no server. Everything you enter stays on your own phone.

Data we collect

None. The app requires no registration or account, and never asks for your name, email, phone number or location.

Where your data is kept

Your tasks, marks, worship records, sleep log, day summaries and every other entry are stored only in your device's storage. They are never sent over the internet and never reach us or any third party.

If you uninstall the app, this data is removed together with it.

Backups

In Settings you may save your data as a PDF file. That file is created on your device and what happens to it is entirely up to you. We neither see nor receive it.

Internet and permissions

The app works fully offline. Android requests the following permissions, each used only for the app's own function:

  • Notifications — to remind you when a task is due
  • Vibration — light feedback when you tap a button
  • Run on boot — to restore reminders after the phone restarts

Nothing gathered through these permissions leaves the device.

Advertising and analytics

There is no advertising. No analytics. No trackers. No third-party services are connected.

Payments

The app is entirely free. Nothing is sold inside it and there are no subscriptions.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, children included.
